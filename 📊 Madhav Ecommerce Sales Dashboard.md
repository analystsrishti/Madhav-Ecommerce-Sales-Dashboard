# 📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Objective

###### The owner of Madhav Store wanted a dashboard to track, monitor, and analyze online sales performance across India. The goal was to gain actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

###### 

## Project Overview

###### This project involves analyzing e-commerce sales data and building an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key business metrics such as sales, profit, quantity sold, customer performance, state-wise sales, product categories, and payment methods.

## 

## Tools \& Technologies Used

* ###### Power BI
* ###### Data Modeling
* ###### DAX Calculations
* ###### Data Cleaning
* ###### Data Visualization
* ###### Interactive Filters and Slicers

## Dashboard Features

* ###### Revenue, Profit, Quantity, and AOV KPIs
* ###### State-wise Sales Analysis
* ###### Customer-wise Sales Analysis
* ###### Category-wise Quantity Analysis
* ###### Profit by Month
* ###### Profit by Sub-Category
* ###### Payment Mode Distribution
* ###### Quarter and State Filters

## Key Insights

#### Sales Performance

* ###### Total Sales Amount: 438K
* ###### Total Profit: 37K
* ###### Total Quantity Sold: 6K
* ###### Average Order Value (AOV): 121K

#### State Analysis

* ###### Uttar Pradesh generated the highest sales.
* ###### Rajasthan and West Bengal were among the top-performing states.

#### Category Analysis

* ###### Clothing contributed the highest quantity sold (63%).
* ###### Electronics contributed 21%.
* ###### Furniture contributed 17%.

#### Payment Mode Analysis

* ###### Cash on Delivery (COD) accounted for 44% of total transactions.
* ###### UPI contributed 21%.
* ###### Debit Card and Credit Card were also widely used payment methods.

#### Profitability Analysis

* ###### Highest profits were observed during January and November.
* ###### Some months recorded negative profits, highlighting opportunities for operational improvement.

## Business Recommendations

* ###### Focus marketing campaigns in high-performing states such as Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan.
* ###### Increase inventory and promotions for Clothing products.
* ###### Optimize operations during low-profit months.
* ###### Encourage digital payment adoption through offers and cashback programs.
* ###### Strengthen relationships with high-value customers.
